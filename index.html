Hi, I'm Juan González.

Computer Engineer with a dual specialization in Information Technologies and Computer Engineering, and two Cybersecurity master's degrees — MUNICS (University of A Coruña) and Deloitte × IMF. Certified as an Automotive Cybersecurity Professional (TÜV NORD) and CompTIA Security+.
